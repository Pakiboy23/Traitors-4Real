# Age rating questionnaire (App Store Connect)

Recommended rating: **12+**.

The app is a draft-and-scoring companion. It depicts no violence, but weekly
calls use the competition’s own terms (“banished”, “murdered”) as labels on
form fields. Combined with a private competitive league, 12+ is the honest
answer. The privacy policy already says the app is not directed at children
under 13.
