Round Table Draft is a private fantasy league for a reality competition season.

Draft a ranked roster before the premiere. Each week, lock banishment and murder calls, play optional bonus games, and watch a live leaderboard reprice after results are certified. Rules are generated from the same scoring pack the board uses, so what you read is what gets awarded.

This is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any television network, studio, or production company, and it is not an official companion to any programme.

What you can do
• Draft ten ranked picks and set season predictions
• Submit weekly council calls before the lock
• Follow live standings, week-by-week movement, and the table leader
• Read every scoring value on the Rules screen

Who it is for
A sealed private league. There is no public social graph, no in-app purchases, and no advertising. Submitting an entry uses your name and email so your score can be attributed to you. Notification reminders are optional.
